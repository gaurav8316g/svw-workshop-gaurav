# Alaska Department of Snow storm procedures

These procedures govern plow dispatch, highway closures, and public alerts.

## Dispatch

Send the nearest available unit from the depot that covers the route. Anchorage covers the Glenn Highway. Fairbanks covers the Parks Highway. Juneau covers local Southcentral closures only when an Anchorage or Fairbanks unit is not available.

Do not dispatch a unit that is on route or in maintenance. Pulling a unit off an active assignment requires duty-officer approval first.

## Road closures

Only a named duty officer can close a state highway. Record the route, the milepost range, the reason, and the duty officer's name.

Glenn Highway MP 40-55 starts open with light snow. Seward Highway MP 90-105 starts closed for avalanche risk. Parks Highway MP 200-220 starts open with ice.

## Public alerts

Write alerts in plain language. Include the route, the mileposts, the condition, and the next review time.

Do not include radio codes, a driver's personal phone number, or a resident's name.

## Priority

Clear school-bus routes and hospital access before other segments when two calls arrive together.

## Privacy

ADS staff use this agency system only. Do not paste storm records into a consumer AI tool.
